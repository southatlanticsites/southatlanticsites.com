Property Overview

About the Property

A ±4.6-acre retail site at the signalized southeast corner of Clayton Blvd (US-70 Bus) and Gordon Rd, just north of the US-70 Bus / I-42 interchange in southern Clayton. Zoned CRM (Corridor Commercial), it is offered for sale at $1M per acre or for ground lease at $90K per acre per year.

The site is planned as four lots of ±2.0, 0.7, 1.0 and 0.9 acres, available individually or combined, with a proposed right-in from Clayton Blvd and full access from Wise Rd. Clayton Blvd carries 22,000 vehicles per day.

Location

  • Southern Clayton just north of the interchange of US-70 Bus (22k VPD) / I-42 (40k VPD)
  • Johnston County is NC's fastest-growing county, up ~19% in five years
  • Near Novo Nordisk's campus and $4.1B expansion, plus Grifols
  • New jobs next door: Beacon's ±310 AC Gordon Road Business Park and Steel 70 (±346K SF)
  • 49,000+ residents and 12,667 employees within 5 mi; $113,518 avg. HH income
  • Near Flowers Plantation, Glen Laurel and Riverwood

Highlights

  • Signalized hard corner on Clayton Blvd
  • Traffic: 22,000 VPD Clayton Blvd | 2,000 VPD Gordon Rd
  • Frontage: ±800' Clayton Blvd | ±225' Gordon Rd | Wise Rd
  • Proposed right-in on Clayton Blvd and full access from Wise Rd
  • 4 lots, available individually or combined
