About the Property

A ±4.6-acre retail site at the signalized southeast corner of Clayton Blvd (US-70 Bus) and Gordon Rd, just north of the US-70 Bus / I-42 interchange in southern Clayton. Zoned CRM (Corridor Commercial), it is offered for sale at $1M per acre or for ground lease at $90K per acre per year.

The site is planned as four lots of ±2.0, 0.7, 1.0 and 0.9 acres, available individually or combined, with a proposed right-in from Clayton Blvd and full access from Wise Rd. Clayton Blvd carries 22,000 vehicles per day.
