Property Overview

6.10 Acres with NC 55 Frontage

Prominently located on the busy NC-55 thoroughfare between US-1 and the NC 540 interchange, this property is easily accessible from all the western Wake County submarkets experiencing explosive growth. With the $3 billion, 1,000 acre Veridea mixed-use development now under way behind the site, the last puzzle piece is falling in place to unlock this location's true potential. As a part of Veridea, the NC Children's Hospital, a partnership between UNC and Duke Health bringing 8,000 jobs and 600 beds, will be built on approximately 230 acres of land at the intersection of US-1 and NC-540.

This opportunity consists of 6.10 acres, already zoned for commercial, with a wide variety of uses allowed including but not limited to drive-through restaurant, grocery, gas, hotel, medical, car wash, and several automotive uses.

Location

  • On busy NC-55 between US-1 and the NC 540 interchange
  • Easy access to the fast-growing western Wake County submarkets
  • Directly in front of the $3B, 1,000-acre Veridea mixed-use development
  • NC Children's Hospital (UNC & Duke Health): 8,000 jobs, 600 beds on ~230 acres
  • Veridea Phase 1: 1,100 homes, 200K SF retail and a 3,000-student Wake Tech campus
  • Veridea plans ~8,000 residential units and 12mm SF commercial space
  • Easements: gas pipeline along northern edge of site

Highlights

  • Zoning: Planned Commercial Conditional Zoning (PC-CZ)
  • Utilities: planned sewer from adjacent property; all others at site
  • Frontage: ~600' on NC-55
  • Access: via NC-55
  • Traffic: 50,000 VPD on NC 55 (NCDOT 2024)
